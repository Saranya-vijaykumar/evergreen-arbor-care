Our Core Mission

Scientific tree care for a safer tomorrow.

We combine advanced diagnostic tools, heavy-duty crane operations, and ecological insight to protect both property investments and natural canopies across the region.

Our Heritage

Born in the Ancient Douglas Fir Forests

Founded in 2004 by ISA Board Certified Master Arborist Marcus Vance, ArborPro Operations originated as a specialized climbing unit tackling hazardous, storm-damaged trees that ordinary landscaping firms refused to touch.

Over two decades, we developed an industry-first hybrid approach: combining the rigorous academic diagnostics of botanical pathology with heavy-duty civil engineering rigging techniques. Today, we manage over 250,000 canopy acres for corporate campuses, municipalities, and private heritage estates throughout the Pacific Northwest.

Field Technology

Diagnostic Scientific Arsenal

We don't guess when assessing living timber. Our teams deploy non-destructive testing tools to reveal unseen internal decay before dangerous failure occurs.

sound_detection_dog_barking

Picrom Sonic Tomography

3D Acoustic Cross-Sections

Sends calibrated sound waves through tree trunks to generate color-coded cross-sectional maps of internal decay and hollow cavities with zero tree harm.

air

Air-Spade Root Decompaction

Supersonic Pneumatic Air Knife

Dislodges dense compacted clay soil at Mach 2 speeds without nicking delicate feeder roots or subterranean irrigation lines.

cable

Dynamic Cobra Cabling

Structural Canopy Bracing

Non-invasive flexible synthetic cables that allow natural tree movement in wind while preventing codominant stems from splitting apart.

Scientific Diagnostics

In-House Soil Science & Pathology Lab

We operate our own dedicated arboricultural pathology laboratory in Seattle, enabling rapid DNA testing of wood rot fungi and comprehensive soil microbiology profiling within 24 hours.

Phase 1

Cambium & Spore Biopsy

Microscopic culture swabs pinpoint destructive fungal pathogens including Armillaria root rot, Verticillium wilt, and Phytophthora.

biotech PCR Pathogen Analysis
Phase 2

Soil Food Web Profiling

Cation exchange capacity (CEC), pH spectrum, and beneficial mycorrhizal fungus counts to formulate custom root nutrients.

science Spectrophotometry Assay
Phase 3

Sonic Acoustic Mapping

Calibrated sound pulses measure wood sound transmission velocities to produce color-coded 2D and 3D cross-sections of internal decay.

graphic_eq Non-Destructive Tomography
Phase 4

Targeted Micro-Infusion

Direct vascular injection of organic systemic fungicides and borer insect deterrents, protecting canopy foliage for up to 3 years.

medication Closed-Loop Trunk Delivery
