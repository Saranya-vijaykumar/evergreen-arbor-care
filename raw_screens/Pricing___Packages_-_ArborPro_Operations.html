Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Are municipal permits included in the quoted price? expand_more

Yes, for all Municipal & Estate packages as well as standard canopy care involving protected species, our team handles local municipal paperwork, permit filing, and compliance checks on your behalf.

How does ArborPro handle insurance claims for storm damage? expand_more

We work directly with major homeowner insurance providers. For emergency hazard mitigation, we document structural breaches with high-resolution photography and provide itemized invoices tailored for swift claims processing.

What are your standard payment terms? expand_more

We require a 30% deposit upon project scheduling for large canopy operations, with the remaining balance due immediately upon satisfactory completion of work. Annual contracts are billed monthly via automated ACH or credit card.

Are your arborists fully bonded and insured? expand_more

Absolutely. ArborPro maintains full liability insurance, worker's compensation coverage, and ISA Certified Arborist credentials across all active field crews to ensure total peace of mind.
