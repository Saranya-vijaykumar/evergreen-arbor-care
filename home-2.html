Incident Command System (ICS)

Severe Weather Rapid Response Protocol

Our documented four-phase mobilization workflow ensures immediate life-safety securing, structural stabilization, and continuous municipal agency liaison.

01 0 – 15 Min

Emergency Call Intake & GPS Triage

24/7 dispatcher cross-references live GIS maps, assesses utility wire proximity, and alerts the nearest heavy crane response unit.

02 15 – 45 Min

On-Site Perimeter & Hazard Containment

Traffic control technicians cordon off danger radius with MUTCD-compliant cones while arborists coordinate with power utility personnel.

03 45 – 120 Min

Precision Crane Pick & Dismantling

Rigging leads secure timber sections under zero tension, lifting up to 6,000 lbs per pick away from roofs, fences, and vehicles.

04 2 – 4 Hours

Biomass Chipping & Site Restoration

Wood chips processed on-site, driveways swept with industrial blowers, and certified TRAQ post-incident report filed for insurance claims.
